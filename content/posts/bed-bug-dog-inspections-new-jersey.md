Cross the Hudson and the bed bug picture looks a lot like the city's, just with a different set of laws and a lot more PATH riders. Hoboken and Jersey City are packed with mid-rise rentals, converted brownstones and new towers filled with people who commute into Manhattan every day. Newark has older multifamily housing, a major airport and a growing hotel market. Up the Turnpike, garden apartment complexes in Bergen and Passaic counties house thousands of families in shared buildings.

All of that adds up to steady demand for bed bug inspections, and steady opportunity for teams that haven't really been tested.

## The commuter effect

Bed bugs don't need a passport, and they don't need much of a ride. The constant flow between Hudson County and Manhattan, on trains, ferries and buses, means a problem in one place can show up in another with very little delay. Property managers on the Jersey side often find that their bed bug calls track the city's.

## What North Jersey buildings demand from a dog

Garden apartments, with long rows of attached units and shared basements, are a different search from a high-rise. So are the converted rowhouses in Hoboken, where an old single-family home might now be four apartments with original plaster and creaky floors. A dog that only knows clean training rooms can struggle in buildings like these.

Tri-State K9 evaluations are run in real multi-room buildings, and teams have to find live bed bug hides they've never seen, in about 15 minutes, while ignoring distractors. It's meant to look like the work, wherever in the region the work happens.

## Same registry, both sides of the river

The Council covers New York, New Jersey and Connecticut with one standard and one public registry. If you manage buildings in Jersey City and Brooklyn, you check teams the same way in both places. Search the registry by handler, dog or team number, confirm the pair matches, and make sure the credential is current.

## Scheduled evaluation days in New Jersey

For handlers based in New Jersey, the Council holds scheduled evaluation days in the region with no travel fee. On-site evaluations at your location are available too, with a travel fee. Check the events calendar for upcoming North and Central Jersey dates.

New Jersey property managers have plenty to worry about. Whether the dog is any good shouldn't be one of them.
