If you own or manage buildings in New York City, you've probably already had the call. A tenant on the fourth floor found something on a pillowcase. Now you need to know how far it's spread, and you need to know before it reaches the units on either side.

That's where a K9 inspection earns its fee. A good team can clear a line of apartments in an afternoon. A bad one can cost you months.

## The problem with "trust me"

The bed bug dog business isn't regulated the way exterminators are. In New York, anyone applying pesticides commercially needs certification from the state Department of Environmental Conservation. There's no state certification for the dog standing next to them. So the quality of the team you hire is entirely on you, the landlord, to check.

Here's what that looks like in practice. An uncertified dog alerts in six apartments. You treat all six, and maybe the units beside them. Two of those alerts were wrong. You've paid for treatment you didn't need, irritated tenants who had to bag their clothes for nothing, and you still don't know whether the real problem is in the basement laundry room the dog walked right past.

Or the reverse happens, which is worse. The dog misses a light infestation in a corner unit. You sign off. Three months later the whole C line has them.

## What certification gives you

A certified team has been tested blind. Somebody else hid live bed bugs and distractor odors in a building the team had never seen, and the team had to find the real hides without alerting on the fakes. The Tri-State K9 standard requires both live hides found and no more than one false response. An alert on a distractor is an automatic fail.

That's a pass-or-fail record you can point to. If a tenant, an attorney or a housing inspector ever asks how you made your treatment decisions, "we hired a team certified within the last 12 months" is a much better answer than "the guy seemed good."

## It's cheap insurance

Checking a team takes almost no time. Ask for the team number, look it up in our public registry, confirm the handler and dog match, and check the valid-through date. You can do it on your phone from the lobby.

Landlords in Brooklyn, Queens and the Bronx already juggle HPD rules, rent regulations and repair schedules. Bed bug detection shouldn't be another gamble. Hire certified teams, and keep a copy of the listing with your inspection records.
