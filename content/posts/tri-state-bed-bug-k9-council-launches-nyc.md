NEW YORK, November 2026. The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council today announced the launch of a regional certification program for bed bug scent detection dog teams working in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. The Council focuses exclusively on bed bug detection and was founded for the tri-state area, with a standard written for the kinds of buildings that define New York City: prewar walk-ups, rent-stabilized multifamily housing, high-turnover hotels and dense student housing.

## Built for the city, not adapted to it

The Council's evaluations take place in real multi-room buildings across the region, so certified teams have proven themselves in conditions that look like the jobs they'll actually do, from a Bronx apartment line to a Midtown hotel floor.

The standard is strict and simple. Each team, meaning one handler and one dog together, searches a building of at least four rooms in about 15 minutes. Evaluators place two live bed bug hides and two distractors out of sight before the team arrives. To pass, the dog must find both live hides with no more than one false response, and an alert on a distractor is an automatic fail. Two evaluators watch every search, and evaluators can't test teams they trained or employ.

Only dogs trained on bed bugs alone are eligible. Credentials last 12 months, so every listed team has been tested within the past year.

## Made with property managers in mind

The Council built its public registry for the people who hire K9 teams: property managers, landlords, co-op and condo boards, hotel operators and university housing offices. Anyone can search the registry by handler, dog or team number and see a team's field evaluation date and expiration in seconds, before a dog ever enters a building.

"Property managers in this city make treatment decisions based on what a dog tells them," the Council said in a statement. "They deserve a quick, reliable way to know the team at the door has been independently tested on live bed bugs, recently, in buildings like theirs. That's what we built."

## Accessible to handlers

The field evaluation is free. Certified teams pay a $225 annual registration. Scheduled evaluation days are held across New York, New Jersey and Connecticut with no travel fee, and on-site evaluations are available at a handler's location for an added travel fee.

## About the Council

The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council is an independent certification body for bed bug scent detection K9 teams in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. It writes the standard, trains evaluators, runs blind field evaluations on live bed bug odor, and publishes a public registry of certified teams. The Council doesn't train dogs or sell inspections. More information, the evaluation calendar and the registry are available at k9council.org. Media inquiries can be sent through the contact form on the website.
