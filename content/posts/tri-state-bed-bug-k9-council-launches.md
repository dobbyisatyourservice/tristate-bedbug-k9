NEW YORK. The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council has launched an independent certification program and public registry for bed bug scent detection dog teams working in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. The Council focuses only on bed bug detection, and its standard is written for the buildings that define the region: New York City walk-ups and high-rises, New Jersey multiple dwellings and shore hotels, Connecticut multifamily housing, and the dorms, schools and shelters found in all three states.

## Built for the Tri-State, not adapted to it

Council evaluations are held in real multi-room buildings across the region, so a team earns its credential in conditions that look like the jobs it will actually do.

The standard is strict and simple. Each team, meaning one handler and one dog together, searches a building of at least four rooms in about 15 minutes. Evaluators place two live bed bug hides and two distractors out of sight before the team arrives. To pass, the dog must find both live hides with no more than one false response, and an alert on a distractor is an automatic fail. Two evaluators watch every search, and evaluators can't test teams they trained or employ.

Only dogs trained on bed bugs alone are eligible. Credentials last 12 months, so every current listing reflects a recent blind field evaluation.

## Made for the people who hire K9 teams

The public registry is built for property managers, landlords, co-op and condo boards, hotel operators, school facilities staff and university housing offices. Anyone can search by handler, dog or team number and see a team's evaluation date and expiration in seconds, before a dog ever enters a building.

The need is especially clear in Connecticut, where state law recognizes a canine team as a qualified bed bug inspector only if it holds a current, independent, third-party certification.

"Property managers across New York, New Jersey and Connecticut make treatment decisions based on what a dog tells them," the Council said in a statement. "They deserve a quick, reliable way to know the team at the door has been independently tested on live bed bugs, recently, in buildings like theirs. That's what we built."

## Accessible to handlers

The field evaluation is free. Certified teams pay a $225 annual registration. Scheduled evaluation days are held across New York, New Jersey and Connecticut with no travel fee, and on-site evaluations are available at a handler's location for an added travel fee.

## About the Council

The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council is an independent certification body for bed bug scent detection K9 teams in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. It writes the standard, trains evaluators, runs blind field evaluations on live bed bugs, and publishes a public registry of certified teams. The Council doesn't train dogs or sell inspections. More information, the evaluation calendar and the registry are available at k9council.org. Media inquiries can be sent through the contact form on the website.
