NEW YORK. The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council is rolling out a new program for New York City this year: local certification for bed bug detection dog teams, and in-field verification for the property managers and businesses who hire them.

Until now, handlers working in the five boroughs have often had to travel out of state to get their dogs certified. The new program brings blind field evaluations into the city, so local companies can certify their teams close to home, in the same kind of buildings they work in every day.

## Certification in the city

NYC handlers and pest control companies can request a Council evaluation for their teams. The standard is strict and simple. Each team, meaning one handler and one dog together, searches a building of at least four rooms in about 15 minutes. Evaluators place two live bed bug hides and two distractors out of sight before the team arrives. To pass, the dog must find both live hides with no more than one false response, and an alert on a distractor is an automatic fail. Two evaluators watch every search, and evaluators can't test teams they trained or employ.

Only dogs trained on bed bugs alone are eligible. Credentials last 12 months, and every certified team is listed in the Council's public registry by team number.

## Verification for property managers and contractors

The second part of the program is built for the people on the other side of the door. Property managers, landlords, co-op and condo boards and hotel operators can now request an in-field verification before a K9 team is approved for their buildings or added to a contract. A Council evaluator confirms, on site, that the dog reliably finds live bed bug odor.

Exterminators who outsource K9 work can use the same service to make sure the team they're subcontracting is the real thing before putting their own name behind its findings.

"In New York City, a lot of treatment decisions start with what a dog tells you," the Council said in a statement. "Property managers and exterminators deserve a simple way to know the dog at the door has been tested on live bed bugs. This program is about keeping the city's K9 community certified and its buildings protected."

## Serving the Tri-State

The Council certifies bed bug detection teams across New York, New Jersey and Connecticut, with scheduled evaluation days throughout the region. The in-city certification and verification program is focused on New York City.

The certification evaluation is free, and certified teams pay a $225 annual registration. On-site evaluations at a handler's or client's location carry an added travel fee.

## About the Council

The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council is an independent certification body for bed bug scent detection K9 teams in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. It writes the standard, runs blind field evaluations on live bed bugs, and publishes a public registry of certified teams. The Council doesn't train dogs or sell inspections. Certification requests, verification requests and media inquiries can be sent through the forms at k9council.org.
