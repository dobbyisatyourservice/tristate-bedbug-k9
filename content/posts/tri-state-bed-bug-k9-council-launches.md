NEW YORK. The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council is rolling out a new in-field verification program in New York City this year. Property managers, hotels, building owners and city organizations that hire bed bug detection dogs can now ask an independent Council evaluator to test the K9 team on site, at the property they want inspected, before the team is approved for their buildings or added to a contract.

For years, the people paying for K9 inspections have had to take the team's word for it. A certificate on a website or a business card doesn't show how a dog performs today, in a building like yours. The new program changes that. Clients no longer have to rely on a K9 company to vouch for itself.

## How in-field verification works

A property manager, hotel or organization submits a request through the Council's website with details about the property and the team they're working with. Any team can be verified, whether or not it's already certified with the Council.

On the scheduled day, a Council evaluator sets blind hides of live bed bugs and distractors in sealed containers at the client's property, out of the handler's sight. The team then searches. To pass, the dog has to find the live bed bug hides and ignore the distractors, under the same criteria the Council uses for its certification standard. The containers are removed after the test, and the client receives a written result.

## Who it's for

The program is built for the people on the client side of the door: property managers and landlords who want proof before approving a K9 vendor, co-op and condo boards setting building policy, hotels, and New York City organizations such as schools, universities, shelters, hospitals and public housing providers that need to confirm a team is legitimate before it works in their buildings.

"In New York City, a lot of treatment decisions start with what a dog tells you," the Council said in a statement. "A team that can't tell live bed bugs from a distractor can mean a needless treatment or a missed infestation. In-field verification protects buildings, residents and guests from teams that aren't what they claim to be, and it helps keep the city's K9 community honest."

## Certification close to home

The Council also certifies bed bug detection teams directly, with blind field evaluations on live bed bugs and a public registry that anyone can search by team number, handler or dog. NYC handlers who have traveled out of state for certification can now be evaluated in the city. The certification evaluation is free, and certified teams pay a $225 annual registration.

## About the Council

The Tri-State Bed Bug K9 Council is an independent certification body for bed bug scent detection K9 teams in New York, New Jersey and Connecticut. It writes the standard, runs blind field evaluations on live bed bugs, verifies teams in the field for clients, and publishes a public registry of certified teams. The Council doesn't train dogs or sell inspections. Verification requests can be made at k9council.org/field-verification, and media inquiries can be sent through the contact form on the website.
