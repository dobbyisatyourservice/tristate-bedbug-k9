New in New York City

In-field K9 verification

Don't take a dog team's word for it. If you're hiring or already working with a bed bug K9 team, a Council evaluator can test that team on site, at the property you want inspected, before you sign a contract or let the dog into your buildings.

1. Request a verification

Tell us about your property and the team you're working with. Any team can be verified, whether or not it's listed in our registry.

2. We test the team at your property

An independent evaluator sets blind hides of live bed bugs and distractors in sealed containers, out of the handler's sight. The containers are removed after the test.

3. You get a written result

You'll know whether the dog found the live bed bug hides and ignored the distractors, using the same pass criteria as our certification standard.

The K9 team to verify

Thanks. Your verification request was received. We'll reply by email with scheduling and pricing.

Who it's for

  • Property managers and landlords approving K9 vendors
  • Co-op and condo boards
  • Hotels and hospitality
  • Schools, universities, shelters and other NYC organizations

Why it matters

Treatment decisions often start with a dog's alert. A team that can't reliably tell live bed bugs from distractors can cost you a needless treatment or a missed infestation.

Already listed?

You can check a team's current credential anytime in the public registry. Field verification goes a step further by testing the team at your property.
