Who we are

Built by Tri-State pest professionals, for one pest

Detection dogs now handle a large share of bed bug inspections in apartments, hotels, dorms and offices across the region. Clients deserve to know that a dog's alert is reliable. Our members are pest management operators, handlers and entomologists who work these buildings every day.

We focus on bed bugs and nothing else. We don't train dogs or sell inspections. We write the standard, train the evaluators, run the tests and publish the results.

How certification works

From application to the public registry

  1. Apply

    Send the team evaluation request with the dog's bed bug odor training log and vaccination records.

  2. Schedule

    Pick a scheduled evaluation day with no travel fee, or request an on-site evaluation at your location with a travel fee. Registration is paid before test day.

  3. Field test

    Your team searches a building of at least four rooms holding two live bed bug hides and two distractors, in 15 minutes. Two evaluators watch; hide locations stay secret.

  4. Get listed

    Pass and you receive a numbered credential and a public registry listing for 12 months.

What it costs

The exam itself is free. Each team pays a $225 annual registration. There's no travel fee at scheduled evaluation days. A travel fee (government mileage rate plus a flat per diem) applies only if you request an on-site evaluation outside a scheduled date.
