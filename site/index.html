Who we are

Built by Tri-State pest professionals, for one pest

Detection dogs now handle a large share of bed bug inspections in apartments, hotels, dorms and offices across the region. Clients deserve to know that a dog's alert is reliable. Our members are pest management operators, handlers and entomologists who work these buildings every day.

We focus on bed bugs and nothing else. We don't train dogs or sell inspections. We write the standard, train the evaluators, run the tests and publish the results.

How certification works

From application to the public registry

  1. Apply

    Send the team evaluation form with the dog's training log and vaccination records.

  2. Schedule

    We match you with an evaluator in your state. Travel costs are paid before test day.

  3. Field test

    Your team searches furnished rooms seeded with live bed bugs, eggs and distractors. Hide locations stay secret.

  4. Get listed

    Pass and you receive a numbered credential and a public registry listing for 12 months.
