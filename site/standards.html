Certification rules (summary)

Draft standard for you to review. Have your board approve the final numbers.

1. Eligibility

Dogs must be at least 12 months old, current on vaccinations and trained on live Cimex lectularius odor. Handlers must be 18 or older.

2. Test layout

Each evaluation uses four to six furnished rooms. Between two and four rooms contain hides of live bed bugs (minimum 5 per vial) or viable eggs. At least one room contains no target odor.

3. Distractors

Rooms include dead bed bugs, cast skins, other common insects, food, and human scent so the team proves it alerts only to live infestations.

4. Passing score

The team must locate at least 95% of hides with no more than one false alert across the full evaluation, within 10 minutes per room.

5. Blind conditions

Neither the handler nor anyone present who can see the handler may know hide locations.

6. Credential

Passing teams receive a credential numbered TSK9-YYYY-NNNN, valid for 12 months. Certification belongs to the team: a new handler or new dog requires a new evaluation.

7. Revocation

The board may suspend a credential for misrepresentation, animal welfare concerns or verified field failures.
