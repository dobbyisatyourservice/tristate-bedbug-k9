Governance

Standards & by-laws

The bed bug scent detection standard every certified K9 team is tested against, using live bed bug odor only, and the by-laws that govern the Council.

Certification rules (summary)

1. We certify teams

Certification belongs to one handler working one dog. If a dog changes handlers, the new pairing is a new team and must be evaluated.

2. Eligibility

Dogs must be at least 12 months old, current on vaccinations, and trained on live bed bug (Cimex lectularius) odor only. Dual-scent dogs are not eligible. Handlers must be 18 or older and may not hold a revoked credential from any certifying body.

3. Test site

A building with at least four rooms: a residence, an office or multi-room interior, or a hotel, dorm or school.

4. Hides

Four hides: two live bed bug hides (5 to 15 live bed bugs each, number set by the evaluators) and two distractors chosen by the chief evaluator. Live hides are placed in separate rooms, and no distractor shares a room with a live hide. Hides are set at least 30 minutes before the search, out of sight of the team. Pseudo-scents are never used.

5. Search and alerts

The search runs 15 minutes, adjusted for building size and agreed before the start. The handler declares the dog's alert style (passive or active), and the dog must alert at the source. Each alert is confirmed by a reward or by the handler calling it.

6. Passing

The team must find both live hides. One false response is allowed, but any response on a distractor is an automatic fail. A dog that won't work may be pulled and retried once within 30 minutes.

7. Evaluators

Two Council evaluators attend every test. The chief evaluator makes the final call, and no evaluator may judge a team they trained or employ.

8. Handling

Harsh corrections are grounds for failure. Pinch collars, electronic or ultrasonic collars, sticks and whips are not allowed during testing.

9. Credential

Passing teams receive a credential numbered TSK9-YYYY-NNNN, valid for 12 months and for bed bug scent detection only. Teams may not claim any other odor under it.

10. Retests and appeals

After a failed evaluation, a team waits 14 days to retest and may not present itself as certified in the meantime. A certified team that fails a retest loses its current credential. Appeals are announced at the test and filed in writing within 14 days; the Certification Chair's decision is final.

11. Revocation

The Council may suspend or revoke a credential for misrepresentation, animal welfare concerns or verified field failures.
