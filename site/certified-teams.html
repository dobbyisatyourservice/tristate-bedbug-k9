Approved training facilities

Facilities whose curriculum meets Council odor and welfare guidelines. Request the Tri-State list through our contact form.

Evaluators

Council-trained evaluators who run field tests. Evaluators may not test teams they trained or employ. Ask about becoming an evaluator.

Sources of live bed bug colonies and training aids that meet our handling rules. Request the list through our contact form.
