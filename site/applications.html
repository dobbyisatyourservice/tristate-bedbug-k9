Applications & forms

Request a team evaluation

Start your team's bed bug scent detection certification or renewal. Your dog will be tested on live bed bug odor in blind hides. We'll confirm your evaluators and test date by email, plus a travel estimate if you request an on-site evaluation.

Thanks. Your application was received and we will reply within two business days.

Before you apply

  • Dog is 12 months or older
  • Dog trained on bed bug odor only (no dual-scent dogs)
  • Vaccination records on hand
  • Training log for live bed bug odor
  • Two sealed live bed bug hide containers for evaluator approval
  • $225 annual team registration paid
  • On-site evaluation outside a scheduled date? Evaluator travel fee paid before test day

Where tests happen

At a scheduled evaluation day, with no travel fee, or on-site at a Council-approved location your team provides, with an evaluator travel fee. Tests may not be run for profit.
